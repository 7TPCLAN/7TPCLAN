Steal an Egg

Movement tricks, boss-avoidance ideas, egg-value priorities and progression tips shared by the community.

Plan your egg run

Know the area’s speed requirement, watch the boss and chasers, and decide how you’ll move before picking up an egg. Game mechanics can change, so treat these as community tips and test them in the current version.

Movement & boss survival

↪️ Wide circles or zigzags

If your speed is below an egg area’s requirement, avoid running in a predictable straight line. After grabbing an egg, try moving in broad circles or zigzags to make it harder for the boss to line up a hit.

Watch the movement tip →

🧱 Wall sticking

A community-reported trick is to stand close to a wall and drop the egg so it clips or sticks into the wall. Players report that this can keep the boss from automatically collecting it if you get punched. This may depend on collision behaviour and could be patched or inconsistent.

Watch the wall-sticking example →

👀 Look back while attacking

When fighting or escaping chasers, briefly turn to face them while attacking, then face forward again to continue moving. Practise the timing so you can land hits without losing your route.

Watch the combat tip →

Economy & egg strategy

🥚 Prioritise size

When the risk is manageable, aim for the largest eggs available. Larger eggs are reported to provide substantially more in-game money and stronger multipliers.

Watch the egg-value tip → · Eggs reference →

🎒 Check eggs through inventory

Instead of placing an egg down just to inspect its type or quality, drag it directly into your inventory. This can save time and reduce the chance of losing it during the check.

Watch the inventory tip → · Related gameplay →

🤝 Ask a fast friend for help

A friend with enough speed may be able to retrieve an egg from a high-tier area, such as the Titan Temple, and bring or drop it in an easier beginner area for you to collect. Coordinate with someone you trust and follow the game’s rules.

📖 Claim Pet Index rewards

Check your Pet Index when you unlock new pets. Claim available index rewards, which can provide useful early-game speed and cash boosts.

Watch the Pet Index tip → · Related guide →

🌙 Use nighttime growth

Players report that eggs can grow much faster during nighttime cycles—up to 30× in some situations. If you are raising eggs, check the current cycle and compare growth in your own game.

Community note: players disagree about whether growth continues beyond the initial hatch timer. Treat the exact timing and multiplier as unconfirmed and version-dependent.

Read the community discussion →

Beginner checklist

🗺️ Learn the map

  • Explore routes and identify the main egg areas.
  • Learn each area’s speed requirement before attempting a run.
  • Read in-game prompts before spending currency or using an upgrade.

📈 Progress steadily

  • Prioritise upgrades that address your current bottleneck.
  • Compare upgrade costs with their benefits.
  • Change one part of your strategy at a time so you can tell what works.
These tips are community-sourced and may change as the game updates. Tricks involving egg placement or collision may stop working after patches. Always keep your Roblox account details private—never share passwords or cookies for a promised boost.
