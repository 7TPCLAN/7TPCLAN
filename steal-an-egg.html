ROBLOX · GAME GUIDE

Steal an Egg

A practical starter guide to learning the game, making safer decisions and improving your runs.

Start with a plan

Take a moment to understand the map, the objective and the risks before committing to a run. Learn the game’s current rules and use each attempt to improve one decision.

Beginner checklist

🥚 Learn the objective

  • Explore the map and identify the main routes.
  • Understand how eggs are collected and what counts as a successful run.
  • Read any in-game prompts before spending currency or using an upgrade.

🛡️ Protect your progress

  • Pay attention to nearby players and changing situations.
  • Avoid carrying out a risky plan when you have more to lose than to gain.
  • Use safe opportunities to learn timing and map awareness.

📈 Progress steadily

  • Prioritise upgrades that help with your current bottleneck.
  • Compare the cost of an upgrade with the benefit it gives.
  • Keep track of what works instead of changing every strategy at once.

🤝 Play fair

  • Use the game’s intended mechanics.
  • Do not trust offers asking for your password, cookies or account details.
  • Report exploits through the game’s official reporting options.

Quick tips

  • Learn one route at a time before trying complicated routes.
  • Watch how the game changes after each round or update.
  • If a strategy fails repeatedly, change one part and test again.
  • Keep your Roblox account secure; never share login details for a promised boost.

Note: This guide intentionally focuses on general strategy. Exact mechanics, upgrades and map layouts may change with game updates.
