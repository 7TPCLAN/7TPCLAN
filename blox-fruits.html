Blox Fruits

Fruit recommendations and progression tips for grinding, travel and PvP. Choose a fruit for the job you need it to do.

Fruit recommendations

GoalFruit to considerWhy
Early-game grindingLightUseful mobility and ranged attacks; especially convenient in the First Sea.
Level grindingBuddhaPopular for close-range grinding when paired with a suitable fighting style.
Sea eventsMagmaStrong damage potential, particularly when awakened.
Travel and mobilityPortalExcellent movement and utility, but not the simplest choice for levelling.
PvP combosDoughCombo-focused option with a high skill ceiling; awakening and practice matter.

These are broad recommendations, not a universal tier list. Fruit balance, awakenings, player skill and updates can change what is best.

Choose by playstyle

⚔️ Grinding

Prioritise reliable damage, area coverage and a moveset that makes quests comfortable. Buddha is a common choice for melee-focused grinding; Light is convenient for early progression.

🌊 Sea events

Look for dependable damage and survivability. Magma is a strong option to consider, especially with its awakening.

🌀 Mobility

Portal is excellent for getting around and utility. It is a travel-focused pick rather than the easiest all-purpose grinding fruit.

🏆 PvP

Choose a fruit that fits your combo timing and preferred range. Dough can be powerful, but it takes practice and a compatible build.

Build basics

Stats

For a fruit-focused build, invest in the stats that support your fruit and survivability. For a melee-focused Buddha setup, prioritise melee and defence. Avoid spreading points across every stat without a plan.

Before changing fruits

  • Check whether your current fruit is helping your immediate goal.
  • Consider mastery and awakening progress before replacing it.
  • Do not trade valuable items based only on pressure from another player.
